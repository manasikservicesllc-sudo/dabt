Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2026

Dabt (“the App”) is operated by MANASIK SERVICES LLC. The App helps organizations manage custody transfers, receiving receipts, and delivery receipts.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, and phone number.
  • Organization information and user role.
  • Barcode and QR code scan results.
  • Receiving and delivery receipt information.
  • Custody transfer and audit history.
  • Photos only when the user explicitly captures or uploads them.
  • Device and push-notification identifiers required for operational notifications.
  • Technical information needed to maintain security and app performance.

How We Use Information

  • Authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • Manage and document custody transfers.
  • Track receiving and delivery receipts.
  • Provide operational notifications.
  • Maintain security, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot the service.
  • Improve application reliability and performance.

Camera and Photos

Camera access is used to scan barcodes and QR codes and, when the user chooses, to capture supporting photos. Dabt does not store camera images merely because a code is scanned. Photos are collected only after an explicit capture or upload action.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the organization that owns or administers the relevant workspace, authorized workspace users, and trusted infrastructure providers needed to operate, secure, and support the service. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as the account or organizational workspace remains active and as reasonably required for custody records, security, legal obligations, and dispute resolution. Data that is no longer required is deleted or anonymized in accordance with our operational and legal requirements.

Data Security

We use secure authentication, encrypted communication using HTTPS, tenant-level access controls, and operational safeguards designed to protect user information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but we continually work to protect the service and its users.

Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law and organizational record requirements, users may request access to their information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion of their account, or information about how their data is used.

Account Deletion

To request account deletion, contact us using the email below from the phone number or email associated with the account. We may need to verify the request. Certain custody and audit records may be retained when required for legal, security, or organizational accountability purposes.

Children’s Privacy

Dabt is intended for organizational and professional use and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International Processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than the user’s country. Where applicable, we use appropriate safeguards for such processing.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect service, legal, or operational changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
